FAQ

Questions teams ask first

Does the AI make the hiring decision?

No. AI screens CVs and scores test answers. Interviewers recommend, and an admin approves each round before the candidate is told.

Do we have to change our email setup?

No. Hire Assist AI reads applications from the mail inbox you already use and replies from it.

How is test integrity maintained during online assessments?

Face detection, tab switches and copy or paste are logged with a snapshot and a time to the millisecond. The test ends automatically after continuous violations.

Where is our data stored?

Your data stays with you, on your own servers or local machines. You also decide where the AI model runs: locally on your infrastructure or as a hosted service.
